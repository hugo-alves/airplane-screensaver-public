Airplane Screensaver

Quick answer

A quiet aircraft radar for your TV: watch nearby planes move across a map, with trails and flight details. An experimental screensaver for rooted LG webOS TVs.

Content reviewed 2026-10-27.

Built for a rooted LG TV with Glasshouse. A browser preview is also available in the project.
